- Serious Fraud Office cases (https://www.sfo.gov.uk/): Not fetched. No location key; cases concern named individuals and companies with no tie to Canary Wharf geography beyond head offices.
- Contracts Finder and Find a Tender (public procurement) (https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/Published/Notices/OCDS/Search?publishedFrom=2026-09-01&publishedTo=2026-09-03&size=5): Answered 200 (OCDS JSON) but is procurement, outside this courts and registers area; the OCDS search has no location filter. - London Tribunals (parking and traffic appeals) (https://www.londontribunals.gov.uk/): Not fetched. Appeals are searchable only by penalty notice and name motorists.
