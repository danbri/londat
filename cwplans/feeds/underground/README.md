# Underground estate: sources found on 2026-10-03

117 sources, 64 recorded exclusions. Four research agents searched for plans, levels and 3D models of the Canary Wharf underground estate: the malls, the stations and the tunnels. Each entry was fetched (or its archive copy was) and its licence was read. The JSON files in this folder are the full records; the tables below are generated from them. Entries marked "3D: yes" give levels or geometry that can be used in the model.

Nothing here is copied into the model except cited level values, which are in `data/sourced-levels.json` with their source and quote. Most plans are copyright of their owners (Canary Wharf Group, TfL, Crossrail): they are for reference by eye, not for copying.
