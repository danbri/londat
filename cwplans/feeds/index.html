Data feeds and APIs: Canary Wharf, the Isle of Dogs, SE16 and the Docklands

Two catalogues: data feeds and APIs (feeds.json, README) and event and real-time feeds: iCalendar, RSS/Atom, REST, what's-on pages and openings and closings signals (events.json, EVENTS.md). Every source below was fetched on the date shown. "Browser" means it needs no key and sends CORS headers, so a web page can call it: press "Try" to call the sample request from your browser now. Only official, published or openly licensed sources are listed; the excluded list at the end says what was left out and why. Re-check: node magpie/cwplans/feeds/check-feeds.mjs and check-events.mjs. What is on and what is closed across the zone (events, licence notices, road works and closures, planned rail, bus and river closures, markets), by date and place: whatson.html. Postcode-keyed results: Canary Wharf postcodes. Everything joined: atlas. Models that use these: Docklands 3D · Canada Water corridor.
