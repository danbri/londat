Canary Wharf postcodes: actual and possible

Every syntactically valid postcode in the E14 district (10 sectors × 400 unit codes), checked against the ONS Postcode Directory (August 2026). "Actual" postcodes are in the directory (live or terminated); "possible" ones are valid codes that Royal Mail has never allocated. Each actual postcode is placed by its ONS grid reference and classified against two definitions of Canary Wharf: the official ward and the 3D model's estate box. Method and limits: README. Data: postcodes.csv · postcodes.json · queries.json · ward boundary. Related: atlas · Docklands 3D · feeds and APIs. Openings and closings

Four official signals from the data on this page. FSA "Awaiting Inspection": a food business that has registered but has not had its first inspection, usually because it is new. Newest ratings: inspections in the last weeks (new or changed premises). New postcodes: Royal Mail allocates them for new buildings or large users. Terminated postcodes: addresses that no longer exist as such.
