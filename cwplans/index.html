Ground, buildings and open track levels are measured (EA LiDAR 1 m). Tunnel levels are not surveyed in any open source. The model joins the levels that are known: portals and open cuts the LiDAR sees (Rotherhithe, Wapping), and published platform depths (Canada Water: 11 m below ground for the East London line, 22 m for the Jubilee line, Wikipedia). The sliders set the shape between them.
