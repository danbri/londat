Canary Wharf building registry

Every OSM building outline in the Canary Wharf box has an internal id (cwb-NNNN, kept between rebuilds) cross-referenced to OSM, Wikidata, postcodes, occupants and owners. Occupants and owners are organisations only. Sources: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), Wikidata via QLever (CC0), FSA food hygiene ratings (OGL), ONS Postcode Directory (OGL). Method and limits: README. Data: buildings.json · ids.json. Related: atlas · Docklands 3D · postcodes · feeds. · data licences.

Also used: OS Open UPRN and Open Linked Identifiers (OGL); GLA London Building Stock Model 2 (OGL); HM Land Registry Price Paid and INSPIRE Index Polygons: "This information is subject to Crown copyright and database rights 2026 and is reproduced with the permission of HM Land Registry. The polygons (including the associated geometry, namely x, y co-ordinates) are subject to Crown copyright and database rights 2026 Ordnance Survey 100026316."; Companies House Basic Company Data. Figures about homes are given only where at least 5 homes are counted.
